Good to know

India Post Payments Bank is an RBI-licensed payments bank: it offers savings accounts only — no fixed or recurring deposits — and caps balances at ₹2 lakh per customer. Deposits are insured by DICGC up to ₹5,00,000 per depositor (principal + interest).
